## 3. Class Information
- Grades Offered: Nursery, LKG, UKG, Grade 1 to Grade 10.
- Board: Maharashtra State Board (SSC Board).
- Medium: English Medium. Marathi is compulsory as per state norms; Hindi is taught as 3rd/2nd language.
- Class Size: 40 students per section, 2 sections per grade. Ratio 30:1.
- Seat availability: Real-time seats must be verified by admission office staff. Never guarantee seat count without office confirmation.

## 4. Age Requirements (Cut-off Date: 31st December 2026)
- **Nursery**: Minimum 3 years complete by 31-Dec-2026 (Born between 01-Jan-2023 and 31-Dec-2023).
- **LKG (Jr. KG)**: Minimum 4 years complete by 31-Dec-2026 (Born between 01-Jan-2022 and 31-Dec-2022).
- **UKG (Sr. KG)**: Minimum 5 years complete by 31-Dec-2026 (Born between 01-Jan-2021 and 31-Dec-2021).
- **Grade 1**: Minimum 6 years complete by 31-Dec-2026 (Born between 01-Jan-2020 and 31-Dec-2020).
- **Grade 2 to 10**: Sequential age with passing TC/LC from previous recognized school.
- Strict Rule: No underage relaxations are permitted under state government regulations.

## 5. Required Documents Checklist
- **For Pre-Primary & Grade 1**:
  1. Original Municipal Birth Certificate + 2 photocopies.
  2. Student Aadhaar Card photocopy.
  3. Parents' Aadhaar Cards photocopies.
  4. 4 passport-size color photos of the student, 1 of each parent.
  5. Residential address proof (Electricity bill / Ration card / Rent agreement).
  6. Caste certificate (if SC/ST/OBC).
  7. Blood group report from a certified lab.
- **For Grade 2 to Grade 10**:
  1. Original School Leaving Certificate (LC / TC) countersigned if from outside Solapur / other board.
  2. Previous standard passing report card / marksheet.
  3. Student & Parent Aadhaar photocopies.
  4. 4 passport photos of student, 1 of each parent.
  5. Address proof & Blood group report.

## 8. Campus Visit & Application Process
- Application Process:
  1. Step 1: Submit enquiry via WhatsApp or collect Admission Form from school office.
  2. Step 2: Submit completed form with photocopies of required documents.
  3. Step 3: Interaction with Principal / foundational readiness assessment (Grade 6-10).
  4. Step 4: Verification of original documents and fee payment at accounts counter.
  5. Step 5: Admission confirmation and issuance of student ID/roll number.
- Campus Visits: Warmly welcomed 10:00 AM to 01:00 PM (Monday to Friday) and 10:00 AM to 12:30 PM (Saturday). Guided walkthrough of classrooms, labs, and playground.

## 9. Admission Policies
- Sibling Priority: Siblings of current HCC students get preference.
- RTE Quota: 25% seats reserved at entry level per Maharashtra RTE rules.
- Refunds: One-time admission fee (₹5,000) is non-refundable. Tuition fee refundable minus ₹1,000 administrative charge only if cancellation is requested in writing before June 1, 2026.
