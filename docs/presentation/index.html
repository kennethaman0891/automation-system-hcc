01 · Institutional Problem Statement

Solving Real Daily School Office & Teacher Friction

Generic school ERPs miss the messy daily reality of Excel markbooks, WhatsApp parent chaos, and morning office logistics.

Operational Scope

📊 Fragmented Teacher Excel Markbooks

Class teachers create multi-sheet markbooks with BOYS/GIRLS divider rows, merged headers, and Excel formulas. Re-uploading often risked overwriting student DOBs or phone numbers.

💬 Unstructured Parent WhatsApp Flood

Parents do not log into portals — they message WhatsApp for admissions, leave notices, fee screenshots, and mark queries. Generic ERPs ignore these unstructured messages.

🕒 7:15 AM Morning & Exam Logistics

Assigning substitute teachers when 3 teachers are absent, building interleaved exam hall seating, and chasing unsigned report cards consumed hours of manual clerical work.

🔒 Privacy & Zero Cloud Lock-In

Student records, phone numbers, and admission chats belong inside the school's own hardware — not leaked to third-party cloud APIs or vulnerable to WhatsApp bulk-ban bots.

05 · Automation #1 — Teacher Markbook Intelligence

Messy Teacher-Excel Auto-Healer & Metadata Shield

Turning error-prone multi-sheet Excel uploads into a guarded, self-healing workflow inside import_excel.php & import_unit_tests.php.

Zero Data Loss Guarantee

1. Smart Structure Detection

Automatically separates F(70) S(30) sub-headers, handles standalone BOYS / GIRLS divider rows without skipping student names, and evaluates Excel =IF(...) remark formulas.

2. Fuzzy Roll-Swap Healing

Compares imported (roll_no, student_name) against the active cohort using similar_text() to flag shifted roll numbers before a single DB row is modified.

3. Metadata Preservation Shield

Teachers rarely keep DOB or parent mobile numbers in markbooks. Overwrite imports automatically preserve existing date_of_birth, parent_phone, and parents_sign.

Blank vs Zero Safety: Empty cells are flagged as unentered instead of silently turning into 0 or AB; exported cells are sanitized via rcCsvSafeCell() against formula injection.
